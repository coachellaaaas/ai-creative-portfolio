@create.withkatheryne is a fully AI-generated lifestyle creator — face, voice, motion, and content. No real person in front of the camera. No studio. Just a carefully assembled pipeline and a series of creative decisions made entirely behind the scenes.

"She doesn't exist — but her audience does."

The project began as pure obsession with what AI tools could actually do in practice. It evolved into something more: a question about authenticity, influence, and identity in an era where a synthetic creator can build a real following, sell digital products, and never have a bad hair day.

About

The

I'm an AI creative with a background in Computer Science and a deep obsession with what generative tools can actually do — not in demos, but in the real world.

My practice sits at the intersection of creative direction and technical experimentation. I don't just use AI tools; I build pipelines, test their limits, and ask what they mean for creativity, identity, and culture.

The Katheryne Gilbert project was my first serious experiment in synthetic identity — building an entirely AI-generated lifestyle persona from scratch and watching what happens when a fictional person starts accumulating a real audience.
